# Luma Foundry Design Directions

### 1. Signal Atelier
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark creative-workflow environment that combines editorial restraint with luminous interface surfaces. The experience makes live creative work feel tangible through bento composition, glass layers, and selective cyan-violet light.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Paper Current
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, print-inspired workspace with oversized serif typography, thumbnail-led stories, and generous ivory space. It would feel like an art magazine designed for a modern production team.

**Probability:** 0.04

## Chosen Direction: Signal Atelier

### Design Movement
**Digital neo-editorialism**: art-directed editorial typography and asymmetry brought into a precision-crafted dark product interface.

### Core Principles
1. Use a **staggered editorial axis** rather than a centered stack: the hero, proof points, and next action should pull across the page with varied visual weight.
2. Let **luminous utility** guide every effect: cyan and violet are signals for focus, live status, and decisive actions, not ambient decoration.
3. Balance **soft glass depth** with hard typographic clarity: blurred surfaces float over a textured dark field, while copy stays sharp and confidently high contrast.
4. Build **progressive discovery**: bento blocks teach the product quickly, while a masonry gallery rewards deeper scrolling.

### Color Philosophy
The base is near-black ink with a faint cool-blue cast, designed to make creative media and bright interface signals feel cinematic rather than saturated. Violet communicates authored possibility; electric cyan communicates active flow. Quiet pearl and fog tones carry essential text and borders, avoiding pure white glare.

### Layout Paradigm
The page uses a **curated production wall**. The hero’s headline occupies an editorial left column while a larger right-side operations canvas breaks the conventional landing-page grid. The feature area converts into a compact bento constellation, and work examples become a multi-column masonry wall with independent image heights.

### Signature Elements
1. **Orbital signal line:** a thin cyan-to-violet gradient path and small nodes that recur in the hero canvas, section labels, and status elements.
2. **Prismatic glass:** translucent cards with controlled blur, low-opacity borders, and a restrained inner light.
3. **Cut-corner markers:** small square indicator details that punctuate cards, navigation, and control groups.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interfaces should feel immediately responsive and deliberate. Controls compress slightly on press, cards lift only enough to reveal hierarchy, and status pills brighten when their associated content becomes active. Every keyboard-reachable element receives a high-contrast focus halo.

### Animation
The hero copy and primary action use a restrained fade-in-up. The operations canvas gains a small staggered entrance; nodes pulse only once after load. Card and button responses use 220–320 ms custom cubic-bezier transitions. Meaningful sections reveal with Intersection Observer, while `prefers-reduced-motion` removes all travel, shimmer, and nonessential pulses.

### Typography System
Use **Space Grotesk** for display moments and navigation, giving the brand a technical yet editorial posture. Use **Manrope** for body copy and data labels for high-density legibility. Display headings may reach high contrast in weight and tracking; supporting copy remains quiet, short-lined, and spacious.

### Brand Essence
**Luma Foundry turns scattered creative work into a live, legible production system for ambitious studio teams.**

**Personality:** precise, luminous, collaborative.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are decisive and image-rich; CTAs name the next artifact or outcome instead of repeating generic product language. Microcopy is brief, helpful, and specific.

Examples: “Make the next good decision visible.” and “Open a live project board.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact **L-shaped prism** formed by two offset luminous planes: cyan at the outer edge, violet on the inner fold, revealing a subtle aperture in negative space. The wordmark pairs a slightly widened geometric “LUMA” with a compact utilitarian “FOUNDRY.”

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Cyan — #5AF4E5**

## Style Decisions

- Every principal section must use a different compositional rhythm while preserving the same ink, glass, and signal-color language.
- Background effects stay quiet and purposeful; readable text and task clarity always outrank visual novelty.
- Glassmorphism is reserved for navigation, the hero workspace, and elevated cards; solid surfaces anchor dense content.
- The template will use bento for product benefits and masonry for work/process stories, keeping source order usable for assistive technology.
- The orbital signal line appears as a recurring cyan-to-violet path-and-node system in every major section, where it indicates flow, status, or progression rather than acting as ambient decoration.
- Every product benefit card includes one compact workflow artifact—a decision, role, state, signal, or preview—so the glass system communicates practical utility as well as atmosphere.
- Abstract project images are paired with named production context, a working phase, and a clear next signal so the masonry wall reads as evidence of creative operations rather than generic visual mood.

## AI SaaS Adaptation: Luma Intelligence

Luma Foundry becomes **Luma Intelligence**, an AI operations layer for product and growth teams. Its positioning is: **Turn your company’s scattered signals into decisions that move the business forward.** The audience is teams that need reliable synthesis across research, customer feedback, product evidence, and changing priorities.

The hero promise becomes **“See the signal. Make the move.”** Instead of abstract creative direction, the visual system will show a premium model-orchestration environment: live sources converging into an evidence-led recommendation, human approval points, and compact agent states. Feature cards will describe **Signal Synthesis**, **Grounded Answers**, **Agent Playbooks**, and **Decision Memory**. The masonry wall will represent AI use cases—customer intelligence, product discovery, launch coordination, and executive briefs—using context labels that explain the operational outcome without claiming unsupported metrics or customer results.

The ink, Signal Cyan, violet accents, prismatic glass, orbital paths, and neo-editorial typography remain unchanged. Imagery shifts from purely studio-like forms to abstract data constellations, layered knowledge planes, and controlled AI-system visual metaphors. The voice remains decisive and precise: it will describe what the product helps teams do, not make unverified performance promises.

## AI Marketer Reference Upgrade

The supplied AI Marketer site is the ground-truth structural reference, with the limitation that its rendered content was not available beyond the page title **“AI Marketer POV.”** The redesigned root page will therefore be an original interpretation rather than a visual replica. It will retain an AI-marketing landing-page goal but raise the craft through an asymmetrical hero, a visible campaign-intelligence workspace, executive-ready story framing, concrete capability artifacts, and a decisive conversion path.

The reference-inspired composition will use **Ember Signal**: a nocturnal editorial performance dashboard that layers inky blue-black space, ember orange, acid-lime confirmation signals, and cool mist text. A heated editorial display face will meet sharp operational UI labels. Signature motifs are a moving campaign pulse line, scored evidence cards, and oversized cut-out score numerals. Headlines will sound direct and practitioner-led, for example: “Stop feeding the funnel. Start reading the signal.” and “Build a campaign brief your entire room can move on.”
