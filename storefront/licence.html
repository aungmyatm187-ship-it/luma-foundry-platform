These are the terms under which a Luma Foundry template is licensed to a buyer. Read them before purchase; purchase is subject to them.

1. Licence variants

Single-use standard

One template for one finished website.

Multi-use agency

One template for up to five finished websites.

Each licence is non-exclusive and non-transferable except as stated. It grants the buyer a worldwide, perpetual right to use the template in the number of finished websites the licence tier permits. The licence does not grant ownership of the underlying source, and the buyer may not resell, sublicence, or redistribute the template itself as a template.

2. What you receive

Upon delivery, the buyer receives the template files, the asset inventory, a setup guide, and a buyer-specific licence reference tied to the release version. Delivery is made available once the order is verified by the merchant of record.

3. Refund policy

Because the product is a digital good delivered immediately, all sales are final once the files are made available. A refund is issued only where the delivered files materially fail to match the described release and cannot be corrected within a reasonable period.

4. Intellectual property

Each template ships with an independent-studio IP declaration: the studio represents that the template and its licensed assets are original or licensed for commercial redistribution, and that any third-party typefaces are replaced with licence-safe equivalents before delivery.

5. Support boundary

The licence includes the release as delivered. Custom work, hosting, and further revisions are separate engagements.

6. Merchant of record

Checkout, payment processing, and tax are handled by the merchant of record (Lemon Squeezy) when activated. Luma Foundry does not store payment details.
