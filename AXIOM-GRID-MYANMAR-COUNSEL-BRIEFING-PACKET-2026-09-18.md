## 5. Myanmar and cross-border questions for counsel

### Contributor and ownership

1. For Myanmar-based contributors, what form of assignment, licence, witness, stamp, notarization, or other execution evidence is recommended for the proposed use?
2. If a contributor works informally or through an unregistered team, who must sign to grant usable rights?
3. If a contractor is outside Myanmar, what governing law, execution, translation, or legalization issues should be considered?
4. Does the proposed document clearly cover commercial template resale, distribution, modification, derivative works, and sublicensing?
5. What evidence is needed where a contribution was created before the current entity or brand was formed?

### Assets and dependencies

6. What licence proof is sufficient for images, fonts, icons, code packages, and other external assets used in a commercial template?
7. Are any asset restrictions incompatible with resale, sublicensing, customization, or customer delivery?
8. What notices/attributions must be included in the product package, website, or customer documentation?
9. Is the partial dependency note sufficient for initial review, or is a formal SBOM/licence schedule required before counsel can conclude?

### Brand and product presentation

10. Should `Axiom Grid` and related visual identity undergo a separate Myanmar and target-market trademark/name search?
11. Does the product copy risk implying a real infrastructure service, SLA, security guarantee, or operational capability when it is an interface/product concept?
12. Are any references, icons, marks, or visual elements likely to create confusion with third-party brands?

### Customer licence and delivery

13. What customer uses should be permitted: one end-client, internal agency use, multiple client projects, or resale?
14. Should the licence distinguish source/template use from the final customized website delivered to a client?
15. What restrictions should apply to redistribution, sublicensing, public source exposure, and inclusion of third-party assets?
16. What refund, support, update, warranty, limitation-of-liability, and dispute terms are appropriate for the intended markets?
17. What tax, invoicing, payment-provider, consumer-protection, and cross-border delivery points require separate professional advice?

### Language and records

18. Should the customer licence be Burmese, English, or bilingual?
19. If bilingual, which version governs in the event of inconsistency?
20. Is a certified translation or other formal translation evidence recommended for contributor agreements or counsel records?
